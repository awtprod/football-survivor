Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This is a private, invitation-only tool for running a personal NFL survivor pool. It has no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your own details before publishing.

Who this policy covers

“Survivor Picks”, “the app”, “we” and “us” refer to the operator of this server — the pool administrator who hosts it for their survivor pool. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to it and to the Terms of Service.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address, and profile photo. This identifies you inside the pool.
  • Your picks and settings. The teams you pick each week, your entry names, reminder preferences, projection settings, and how many entries you have.
  • Push subscription data. If you enable reminders, the app stores the browser-generated push subscription (endpoint and keys) and your device's user-agent string, so it can deliver notifications to that device.
  • Pool workbook data. The pool administrator uploads the pool's spreadsheet of entries and past picks. It may include other participants' entry names and pick history. That data is used only to model the field and grade picks — it is not exposed beyond what pool members see in the app.
  • Sign-in cookies. One session cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax) keeps you signed in, plus a short-lived cookie during Google sign-in handoff. There are no advertising or tracking cookies of any kind.

What we do not collect

No analytics or usage tracking, no advertising identifiers, no location data, no payment information (the app handles no money), and no special-category data.

Third parties

  • Google — handles sign-in (“Sign in with Google”). Google receives the sign-in request and its own privacy policy applies to that exchange. The app never posts anywhere on your behalf.
  • Your browser's push service (e.g. Chrome/Firebase, Apple) — delivers reminder notifications. Reminder payloads contain only the week number and a nudge; your picks are not included.
  • ESPN and public sports-data sources — the server fetches schedules, results, odds, and team logos so it can grade picks and build projections. Requests to these sites are made by the server, not your browser, and contain none of your data.
  • SurvivorGrid — the server may fetch public consensus data from it, again server-side and anonymously.

How data is stored and kept safe

Everything is stored in files on this server, which runs over HTTPS and is reachable only to people the administrator has allow-listed. Access is limited to the administrator and, for your own data, to you. No data leaves the server except as described above. There is no automated retention: data is kept while your account is active.

Your choices

  • Sign out from Settings → Account on any device.
  • Disable reminders any time from Settings → Push notifications, or revoke the browser permission.
  • Delete your account. Ask the administrator and they will remove your account, picks, settings, and push subscriptions. Ask them also if you want a copy of your data.
  • Access & correction. You can see and edit everything the app holds about you in Settings. Entry names that come from the pool workbook are corrected in the workbook by the administrator.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Do not use it if you are under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).
